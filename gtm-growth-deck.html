01 — The problem you already know

The answer exists. It's just
scattered across six systems.

A founder asks a simple question: "why did we lose that deal?" To answer it honestly someone opens the CRM, the billing tool, a usage report, then hunts for the call recording and searches the inbox. That's about an hour. So most of the time, nobody asks.

appsDifferent systems6 each with its own login, its own search, its own idea of "customer"
descriptionFormats7+ tables, transcripts, email threads, slides, PDFs, video, spreadsheets
scheduleRealistic answer time~1hr so the question quietly stops being asked

The data isn't missing. It's unreachable.

02 — Why teams talk past each other

Everyone sees a slice.
Nobody sees the customer.

campaignMarketing sees

Clicks, form fills, MQLs, pipeline sourced. It knows who showed up, not what happened on the call.

handshakeSales sees

Deals, stages, objections, close dates. It knows why a deal stalled, but it lives in notes nobody reads.

support_agentCustomer success sees

Usage, renewals, complaints. It knows who is quietly drifting away long before the renewal date.

account_balance_walletFounders see

Revenue and a board deck. A tidy summary of a story they never got to read.

Why it matters"Marketing leads are weak" and "sales doesn't follow up" are often the same disagreement, argued from two partial views. The fix isn't another meeting. It's one place where everybody can ask the same question.

04 — The idea

One question. Every source.

Before UPI, your money wasn't missing. It was just hard to reach: six banks, six apps, six logins. UPI didn't pool anyone's money. It put one interface over every bank. This is the same move for your go-to-market data. Nothing gets copied into a giant new tool. You type an ordinary sentence and it goes and gets the answer.

Why it mattersThere is nothing new to learn. The question is the interface. A founder, a marketer and a rep can type the same sentence and each get a useful answer.

06 — For marketers

Stop guessing your messaging. Read what buyers said.

Thousands of sales calls are a free, unfiltered focus group. Search them by meaning, not keywords. Search "budget concerns" and it finds "this is too expensive" and "I need finance to sign off", words the customer actually used.

edit_noteMessaging

Lift the buyer's own phrases into headlines, ads and landing pages instead of ours.

articleContent

The questions asked most on calls are the blog posts and FAQs you haven't written yet.

compare_arrowsCompetitive

See who comes up, in what context, and what makes buyers lean one way or the other.

Why it mattersCampaign dashboards show what people clicked. Call transcripts show what they actually worried about. Your next campaign angle is probably already in last month's calls.

07 — For account-based teams

An account is really a group of people

Open a company record and it looks nearly empty. The history is scattered across the individual people who work there. Following that trail by hand is why account research takes so long.

09 — For founders

You don't need a data team. Start with three things.

looks_onePick two sources

Your CRM and your call recordings. Two sources already answer most of "why did we win or lose?"

looks_twoPick one weekly question

"What did customers push back on this week?" Ask it every Monday and watch the pattern build.

looks_3Decide who sees what

Set access rules on day one. A rep doesn't need revenue figures. Easier to start strict.

Why it mattersWe run six sources at the scale of thousands of calls, but that wasn't day one. Clean CRM habits and recorded calls are what actually make this work, far more than any particular tool.

10 — How it connects

The assistant gets tools, not keys

The standard behind this is called MCP, think "UPI for AI". Your UPI app never learns your bank password. It is handed a short list of things it may do. The assistant works the same way: it can use a tool, it cannot reach into your systems.

smart_toyClaudeasks in English
→
hubThe connectora short list of named tools
→
dnsYour systemsread-only · checked

Why it mattersIf a tool was never built for something, the assistant simply cannot do it. That is how it reads real customer data without ever holding real access to the database.

11 — Trust

"Is our customer data safe?" The three rules.

visibilityIt can only read

A read-only login. It cannot edit a deal, delete a contact or send an email on anyone's behalf.

list_altOnly what we listed

Every table and column is named in advance. Anything not on the list is unreachable.

filter_altDifferent people, different answers

Revenue figures are kept away from the general channel. A rep and a finance lead see different things.

Why it mattersTwo people can ask the same question and correctly get different answers. The rules live in the connector, so rewording the question doesn't get past them.

12 — Takeaways

Four things worth taking home

forumYour calls are your best research

Thousands of conversations already hold your messaging, objections and competitors. Start reading them.

hubConnect before you add

You rarely need another tool. The questions worth asking cross the ones you already own.

loopShorten the loop

Make learning from sales calls weekly, not quarterly. That is where the real advantage sits.

securitySet the rules first

Decide who sees what before the first question is asked. It is much harder to add later.

13 — The questions we always get

Asked and answered

engineeringGetting started

Do I need a data team to do this?

Not to start. The hard part isn't the AI. It's tidy CRM habits and recorded calls. The engineering is a one-time job, and the people asking never touch it.

psychologyAccuracy

How do you stop it inventing numbers?

It never writes free-form queries, so an invented column errors instead of returning something plausible. And every answer shows its working, so you can check it.

paymentsCost

What does it cost to run?

Running it is modest. The real cost is the one-off indexing of calls and emails so they can be searched by meaning. After that it grows only as new data arrives.
